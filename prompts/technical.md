### Workstream focus: technical

Research architecture, infrastructure, technologies, engineering decisions,
implementation patterns, APIs, security, scalability, reliability and the
operating model. Prefer official documentation, engineering blogs of the
organisations involved, conference talks, papers and specifications.
Distinguish what an organisation has said it does from what vendors claim
about it.

- **Architecture.** Where a system's architecture is documented, capture its
  components, their grouping (layer, plane or tier) and how they connect —
  direction and what flows along each connection — precisely enough for a
  diagram (in `evidence`).
- **Performance.** Record benchmark and performance figures (latency,
  throughput, utilisation, cost per unit, scale limits) with their conditions:
  hardware, software version, configuration, workload and who ran the test.
  Vendor-run benchmarks are tier 3 and must say so in `notes`. Figures
  measured under different conditions are not comparable; note it.
- **Maturity.** Record version and status (alpha, beta, GA, deprecated) with
  release dates, so feature maturity can be tabulated or put on a timeline.
