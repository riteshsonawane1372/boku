### Workstream focus: market and industry

Research market structure, size and growth, adoption rates, trends, business
models and industry economics. Market-size figures vary widely between
analysts: record who produced each estimate, its date, its definition of the
market, its base year and forecast horizon, the CAGR if stated, and whether it
is a forecast (`kind: estimated`). Record each estimate as its own finding;
never average estimates from different firms into one number, and never mix
them in one series. Survey results need the publisher, sample size,
population, geography and fieldwork date; record year-on-year results of the
same survey as a series. Where shares or rankings are published (vendor
share, adoption by segment or region), capture every listed value with its
unit so they can be compared in one chart.
