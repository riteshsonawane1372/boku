### Workstream focus: primary sources

Find first-party and official evidence: company filings and investor
materials, regulatory and government publications, official documentation,
release notes, standards, academic papers and statistics agencies. Your
findings anchor the report, so favour tier 1 sources and record exact
figures, dates and document names (form type, section, table or page where
useful). When an official source publishes a data table, capture the full
series, not only the latest value. Record effective dates of regulations,
standards and releases so they can form a timeline. If a claim is only
available in secondary coverage, say so in `notes`.
