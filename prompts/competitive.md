### Workstream focus: competitive intelligence

Identify the relevant competitors and compare positioning, capabilities,
product strategy, pricing (only where publicly listed, with date, currency and
pricing unit) and evidence-backed strengths and weaknesses. Every comparative
statement needs a source; "leading" or "best" requires a measurable basis.
Prefer vendors' own documentation for capabilities and independent sources
for performance or market share claims.

Research each competitor against the same dimensions so the results form a
comparison table: record one finding per competitor per dimension (e.g.
"Vendor A: supports X since v3.2 (2025-06)"), using identical wording for the
dimension across competitors. Where a capability is absent from official
documentation, say "not documented" rather than "not supported".
