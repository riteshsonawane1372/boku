### Workstream focus: financial and business

Research revenue, costs, margins, growth, valuation, funding, unit economics
and return on investment. Use filings and official results first. For every
figure record currency, unit (thousands, millions, billions), period (fiscal
vs calendar year, and the fiscal year end), and whether it is GAAP/IFRS or
adjusted. Capture multi-period series (at least three periods where
available) and segment breakdowns from the same filing so trends and mix can
be charted. Mark analyst figures `estimated`; mark anything you calculate
(growth rates, margins, CAGR, per-unit costs, break-even points) `derived`
and show inputs and arithmetic in `evidence`. For cost or ROI models, list
every assumption with its source, and note which assumptions the result is
most sensitive to. Do not convert currencies or adjust for inflation unless
you state the rate, its date and source.
