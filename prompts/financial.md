### Workstream focus: financial and business

Research revenue, costs, margins, growth, valuation, funding, unit economics
and return on investment. Use filings and official results first. For every
figure record currency, period (fiscal vs calendar year), and whether it is
GAAP/IFRS or adjusted. Mark analyst figures `estimated`; mark anything you
calculate `derived` and show inputs and arithmetic in `evidence`. Do not
convert currencies or adjust for inflation unless you state the rate and
source.
