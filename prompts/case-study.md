### Workstream focus: case studies

Find concrete, named, verifiable real-world implementations relevant to the
objective. For each case capture, as separate findings with sources: context
(organisation, sector, scale), the problem, constraints, the approach taken,
the technology or operating model, challenges, results and lessons. Use
`topic` = "Case: <organisation>" for all findings of one case.

Record results as measurable before/after figures with units, the baseline,
the measurement period and who measured them ("p95 latency fell from 820 ms
to 310 ms between 2025-01 and 2025-06, per the company's engineering blog").
Record key dates of the implementation (start, pilot, production, expansion)
so a timeline can be drawn. Where the architecture is described, capture its
components and connections as the technical workstream would.

Vendor-published customer stories are acceptable but must be labelled as such
in `notes`; prefer the organisation's own account. Do not construct composite
or hypothetical cases.
